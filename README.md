Murmur turns your voice into text on your Windows computer. It works offline. You keep your data private. It uses your computer hardware to process speech. No audio leaves your machine. You press a key, speak, and the words appear in your active program.

## 💻 System requirements

Murmur needs specific hardware to work well. Your computer must run Windows 10 or Windows 11. 

If you want the fastest results, use a computer with an NVIDIA graphics card. The software uses the CUDA platform to speed up transcription. If your computer lacks an NVIDIA card, Murmur uses your processor. This process takes more time but still works.

Ensure you have at least 8 gigabytes of memory. You also need about 2 gigabytes of storage space for the speech models.

## 🚀 Getting started

Once you install the software, open it from your start menu. The program opens a small control window. You can leave this window open or minimize it to your system tray.

To dictate:
1. Click into the box where you want your words to go. This could be a document, an email, or a web browser.
2. Press and hold your chosen keyboard key. The default key is usually set to the F8 button.
3. Speak clearly into your microphone after you hear the activation sound.
4. Release the key when you finish your sentence.
5. The program transcribes your speech and pastes the text directly into the active window.

## 🔒 Privacy and security

Murmur handles all audio processing locally. It does not connect to the internet to transcribe your words. No company stores your recordings. Your voice stays on your computer at all times. This provides a secure way to dictate confidential documents or personal emails.

## 🛠️ Troubleshooting

If the program fails to transcribe your words, check these points:

- Check your microphone connection. Ensure Windows recognizes your device.
- Verify that your graphics card drivers are current if you use the NVIDIA mode.
- Close programs that use your microphone to avoid conflicts.
- Restart the program if it stops responding to your key press.

If you continue to have trouble, ensure your computer meets the minimum hardware requirements. Low memory can cause the program to close without warning. Close other heavy programs to free up resources for the transcription engine.
